    The race

    • Finish in the top 3 to qualify. Qualify on all five roads to reach the next level.
    • Every finish pays prize money, plus a bonus for each rider you knock off.
    • Ride up beside a rival and hit them until their bar empties. Knock off an armed rider while your hands are empty and you take their club or chain.
    • A kick does little damage but shoves a rider sideways, into traffic or off the road.
    • Crash too often and your bike is wrecked. You pay for repairs.
    • Cops chase anyone they see. Get caught on the ground near one and you pay a fine.
    • Run out of cash and your career is over.
